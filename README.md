# KeenKeper

Friendship Tracker is an easy-to-use and engaging web application designed to help users organize and monitor their interactions with friends. It allows users to record calls, messages, and video chats, explore a timeline of their activities, and gain insights into their communication habits through visual charts.

## ✨ Core & Analytics Features

### 📊 Interaction Analytics & Visual Insights
* **Dynamic Data Visualization:** Transforms communication data into beautiful, easy-to-understand charts, providing deep insights into personal communication habits and frequency.
* **Activity Breakdown:** Beautifully maps out interaction types (Calls, Messages, Video Chats) to show users where they invest most of their social time.

### ⏳ Interactive Social Timeline
* **Chronological Activity Log:** A beautifully organized, sequential timeline that tracks every past interaction, making it effortless to revisit memories and catch-up histories.
* **Milestone Tracking:** Highlights key moments and consecutive touchpoints with friends to help maintain and nurture long-term relationships.

### 📝 Smart Interaction Logger
* **Granular Log Management:** A seamless interface allowing users to record, update, or remove interaction logs, including custom notes about the conversations.
* **Multi-Channel Categorization:** Built-in tagging system for various communication mediums (e.g., Audio Call, Video Chat, Texting, or In-person Meetups).

### ⚡ User-Centric Design & Experience
* **Minimalist & Engaging UI:** Designed with a clean, focused, and distraction-free interface to make personal tracking an enjoyable daily habit.
* **Fluid Responsiveness:** Fully optimized layout that ensures smooth tracking and chart rendering on everything from mobile screens to desktop dashboards.
